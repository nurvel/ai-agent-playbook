# Product Management Principles

## Summary
Product work should clarify the problem, tradeoffs, and acceptance signals before implementation details take over.

## Guidance
- Clarify the user, problem, value, and timing before committing to a solution.
- Separate discovery, requirements, roadmap, backlog, and implementation handoff.
- Keep roadmap work strategic and backlog work operational.
- Requirements should be observable and testable.
- Drop or defer work when the value, risk, or timing does not justify it.
