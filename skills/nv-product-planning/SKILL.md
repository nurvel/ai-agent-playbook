## Scope

- Work on the requested stage: discovery, requirements, or slicing. Preserve established decisions and skip stages that add no value to the request.
- Focus on a feature's purpose, behavior, and scope. Portfolio priorities and technical implementation plans are separate tasks.
- Separate confirmed facts, assumptions, decisions, and unknowns. Ask about missing information only when it materially affects the result.

## Discovery

- Identify the affected users, current pain, desired outcome, and why the problem matters now.
- Compare meaningful options, including doing nothing, before settling on a solution.
- Make value, constraints, risks, and reversibility visible. State what still needs validation; do not treat an explored idea as an approved requirement.

## Requirements

- Define in-scope behavior, non-goals, dependencies, and relevant data, content, UX, or operational constraints.
- Write observable, testable acceptance criteria. Include realistic edge cases and non-functional requirements where they affect acceptance.
- Keep unresolved decisions explicit. Describe needed behavior without prematurely prescribing technical implementation.

## Story slicing

- Split agreed scope into small, testable slices that deliver user value. Avoid separating stories solely by technical layer.
- Distinguish the MVP flow from later enhancements; make dependencies and useful delivery order explicit.
- Give each story a goal, scope, acceptance criteria, and dependencies. Keep a standalone story breakdown usable without a separate handoff document.

## Output

- Return the requested analysis, scope, or story list in chat unless a file update is requested. Include material open questions and the next useful decision or validation step.
- Follow the target project's existing tracker and artifact formats. Avoid creating a parallel source of truth.
- For an Idea Brief, Requirement Refinement, or Product Spec without an existing format, use the relevant shape in [artifact templates](references/artifacts.md). Read only the template needed for the requested output.

## Check

- Does the result answer the requested stage without adding unnecessary process?
- Are scope, evidence, assumptions, and acceptance clear enough for the next step?
- Do proposed stories preserve the agreed outcome and expose dependencies?
